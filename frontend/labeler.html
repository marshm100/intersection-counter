Articulated (A, orange) = a cab pulling a SEPARATE trailer (18-wheeler; visible hinge, trailer has its own wheels).
Long single-unit (L, blue) = inherently ~2+ car lengths on ONE chassis: box truck, U-Haul, dump truck, bucket truck, school bus, RV.
Medium (M, pink) = commercial 2-axle-plus truck or bus that is NOT 2+ car lengths: bobtail tractor, delivery/service truck, shuttle bus.
Everything else (incl. pickup+trailer, pickups, vans) = vehicle.
