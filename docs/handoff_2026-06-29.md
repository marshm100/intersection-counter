## Principles to carry forward
- **Blind deployment.** Ground truth (Miovision/manual) is DEVELOPMENT data — learn
  failure modes once, generalize. NEVER a deployment dependency. Do NOT per-site-overfit
  knobs (the corridor's per-camera tuning is this risk).
- **Net is not enough** — per-interval (AVG |err|) is the bar.
- **Cardinal = position; approach = opposite** (see `backend/services/cardinals.py`); turn
  left/right/through is convention-invariant.
