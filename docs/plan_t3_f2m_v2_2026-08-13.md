## The one mechanism change

An origin (or destination) leg is DISTRUSTED for constraint purposes iff
the window's own strict-full census marks it flip-prone:
`confusion[leg] > 0.25` — one in four of that leg's own gate-VERIFIED
full journeys has early points sitting nearer a rival origin's channel
family (the existing, blind, per-window metric built for exactly this in
`two_pass.strict_full_census`; consumed today by V2_MERGE_RESCUE's
saturation logic). For an entry_only fragment whose origin is
distrusted, the entry-mode constraint is DROPPED: the fragment scores in
free mode (all points, all cells) and the prototypes adjudicate the
origin too. exit_only symmetric on a distrusted destination. Trusted
legs keep the pilot's exact behavior. Nothing else changes: same
prototypes, same self-calibrated floors, same guards, same composition.
The distrust threshold 0.25 is declared here, not swept; per-window
confusion values are REPORTED per leg in the diagnostics.
