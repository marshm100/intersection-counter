## Key honest findings (don't re-litigate)
1. **Single-digit was a 30-min AM cherry-pick.** cam1 7.2 / cam2 8.8 / cam5 7.7 were all AM-peak 30-min figures. Full-study (denser midday/PM, far more vehicles → more absolute error) is inherently teens for cam1/2/5. Single-digit-over-full-study only reachable on cam3/cam4. Recipes still ~halve every camera's error.
2. **cam2 (25fps) REQUIRES `pre_track_nms_iou=0.85`** on its throughs — disabling it caused a 25–32% regression. Per-camera persisted calib must be respected, not blanket-overridden.
3. **Detection is NOT the binding constraint** (P0). The levers are tracking/attribution/calibration.

## NEXT — what we intend to do (the two live threads, opened 2026-06-19)
### A. cam2/cam5 calibration (user suspects they were never properly calibrated — CONFIRMED partial)
Leg-sanity (calibrated reference_heading vs observed entry bearing, from gtfree QA) shows real offsets on exactly these two cameras:
- cam2: EB **33°** off, NB 19°, WB 13°, SB 3°.
- cam5: WB **28°** off, NB **24°**, SB 10°, EB 9°.
Not the 180° cam1-class swap, but 20–33° on cross-street/NB legs hurts classification + bank matching. ## Gotchas / how we work here
- **Accuracy = full-study windows now**, not 30-min AM. State both when reporting.
- **OneDrive file locks** → all scratch/side DBs in system temp (`scratch.py`), never the project dir.
- **ECC warning spam**: botsort retracks flood the log with "ECC did not converge"; grep past it. `evaluations/*.log` is gitignored (tens of MB).
- **Background retracks**: bytetrack is silent (no per-frame log); judge "still running" by side-DB mtime + process CPU, not the log.
- **Weekly reports**: engaged session spans (not raw duration), specific numbers (not laymen metaphors). weekly-reports/YYYY-MM-DD.txt (end-of-range).
- Detection caches (study_*.parquet) + ReID sidecars (study_*.reid.npz) live under `data/` (gitignored). Don't delete — rebuilding them is the days-long part.
