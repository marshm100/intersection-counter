## A4a — production turn-merge on the replay output + S5 emitter

**Move the mechanism into the backend** (`backend/services/turn_merge.py`):
port `merge_turn_fragments` + `borderline_merge_cells` from
`scripts/hybrid_ocbot.py` verbatim (scripts re-import from the service for
compat — one source of truth). New post-pass on a replay DB:
`merge_replay_turns(project_id, camera_id, db, window)`:
1. Load the camera's turn events from the replay DB (same-cell fragments).
2. `expected_by_cell` = the applied bank's `supporting_count` per (origin,
   dest) — **the blind gate** (validated 2026-07-08 on cam1: the accepted
   GT-free recipe) — **scaled to the counting window**:
   `expected × (window_seconds / bank_sample_seconds)`.
   - Sample-window persistence is the missing piece: add nullable
     `sample_window_seconds` to `intersection_paths` (migration + writers in
     build_bank_gtfree/apply paths). Corridor backfill = 1800 s (the banks
     were 30-min samples — recorded as an assumption in the migration note).
     A §3-A corpus-built bank sets it to the corpus window → scale 1.
3. Mark merged-away fragments `rejected=1` (non-destructive, reviewable),
   keep-longest per group — the existing mechanism, zero constant changes
   (merge_px 30, merge_gap 40, vol_factor 1.3 stay frozen).
4. **S5 emitter:** `borderline_merge_cells(...)` results →
   `review_flags` rows (kind suspected_gap, subtype `merge_borderline`,
   impact = |raw − expected|) via `insert_flags`. Closes the S5 deferral;
   the flagqueue retrospective's T4 flips CAUGHT at the next B6 run.
