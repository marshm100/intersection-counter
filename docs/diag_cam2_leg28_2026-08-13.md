# Diagnostic — cam2 leg 28 (EB) false-origin anatomy
# (2026-08-13; prep for the operator recalibration session)

Not a mechanism block — instrumentation. F2M-v2 measured leg 28 at
**0.981 self-confusion** (98% of its own gate-verified fulls carry
rival-shaped early geometry). Before the operator redraws the leg-28
mouth, this diagnostic answers the three questions that decide WHAT to
redraw — and whether a redraw can work at all:

1. WHERE along the leg-28 gate line do inward crossings happen —
   position distribution of TRUE-EB crossings (fulls whose early points
   fit EB channels) vs FALSE crossings (fulls fitting rival channels),
   plus the fragments' crossings. If the two populations separate
   spatially, a line/zone redraw fixes it; if they overlap, no geometry
   fixes it and the remedy is crossing LOGIC (a new mechanism block).
2. HOW FAST are the crossings — queue-creep produces slow, jittery,
   shallow crossings; real EB entries cross at approach speed. A speed
   split that separates cleanly is an alternative (or additional)
   operator-facing lever.
3. WHAT the current geometry is — the drawn mouth segment, its length
   and orientation, so the redraw guidance is concrete.
