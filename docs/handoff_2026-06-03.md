## What was fixed (root causes, applied)
1. **Stage 1 — bank snap-magnet (attribution).** Rare-turn bank paths stole throughs (cam4 EB-left:
   manual 6, attributed 214). Two **self-limiting, data-driven** gates, no per-camera flags:
   - `build_bank.py` MAGNET GATE: reject a TURN polyline that is straight (>0.95) AND over-supported
     (support > 3×manual). Kills cam5's NB-right magnet (support 562 vs 5); spares real turns.
   - `pipeline.py` ORIGIN-REWRITE GATE (`config.PRE`… `ORIGIN_REWRITE_GATE_*`): reject a joint-scorer
     turn match that rewrites origin to a non-nearest leg, ONLY when the track is straight (≥0.97).
     Real turns curve, so cam1's mid-turn entries are spared.
   - Memory: [[project_accuracy_snap_magnet_mechanism]].
2. **Stage 2 — stale shipped throughs.** `apply_hybrid` always KEPT project.db's bytetrack throughs and
   only swapped turns, so throughs were the pre-Stage-1 reprocess output — cam5's were badly duplicated
   (812 vs 650). FIX: regenerate via a fresh bytetrack retrack (`apply_bank --backend bytetrack`) and feed
   it in with `apply_hybrid --throughs-db`. cam5 22.3→9.5%. **PER-CAMERA**: helped cam5, REGRESSED cam4
   (its shipped 728 throughs beat a fresh retrack 653) — so cam4 was left as-is. Memory:
   [[project_accuracy_stage2_throughs]].

## The real remaining levers (tracking/calibration, NOT detection)
1. **cam5 dead EB approach** (EB-right 39→1): **FIXED & APPLIED 2026-06-03 (commit 6ec8a65). cam5 9.5%→7.7%,
   EB-right 1→16.** Root cause was `build_bank`'s nearest-anchor endpoint grouping silently dropping the
   38→39 cell (botsort tracked 36 EB-rights but binned all to (39,39)). Fix is GENERAL: when nearest-anchor
   leaves a real Miovision cell under-supported, recover it by re-binning on direction-of-travel vs each
   leg's reference_heading. Recover-only → no-op where anchors suffice (cam3) → no regression. Clean A/B
   (bank ± just the 38→39 path) = EB-right 1→16, cam5 8.9%→7.7%, zero collateral. See
   [[project_cam5_ebright_recoverable]]. **Residual follow-ups:** (a) EB-right caps at 16/39 — the RETRACK's
   polyline attribution (score_origin_by_polyline) matches only 16 of ~36 botsort EB-right tracks; tune
   max_avg_distance_px / bearing gate / the recovered polyline for more. (b) cam5 WB-right (36→37) over-counts
   27 vs manual 6 — PRE-EXISTING snap-magnet-lite, independent of this fix (identical in both A/B arms).
2. **cam2 / cam5 through-duplication**: multi-mechanism (double-box + ID-switch). NMS@0.85 helps cam2;
   the `lost_track_buffer`/match tracker knobs would need PIPELINE PLUMBING (they conflict with

## Gotchas / how we work here
- **Per-camera measurement rules everything** — every "global" lever flipped sign on some camera (fresh
  throughs, NMS, ReID, dedup). Always measure per camera before applying.
- **The stale-throughs trap**: `apply_hybrid` keeps project.db throughs unless `--throughs-db` is passed.
- **cam2 is only processed at 5-min in project.db** — any 30-min cam2 work needs a retrack (and its 25fps
  bytetrack duplicates: 30-min SB→NB 393 vs manual 310).
- OneDrive locks ([[reference_onedrive_locks]]): scratch in temp now; never write scratch to `_hybrid_tmp`.
- Diagnostic-first, data-driven, robust — engineer visual gate + atomic backup before any project.db apply.
- Metric = net @ TMC granularity vs Miovision; per-minute gross is unreachable (timing floor). ≤1% is
  aspirational on busy cameras and bumps Miovision's own noise floor ([[project_accuracy_target_metric]]).
