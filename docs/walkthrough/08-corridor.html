8. The Corridor

The Sunnyvale study isn't one intersection — it's five, strung along N Belt Line Rd, one camera each. Everything in pages 1–7 was written once and parameterized by --camera N; this page is how a single-camera pipeline scales to a corridor without per-camera code.

Note the methods differ per camera — that's the per-camera-measurement discipline (page 7), not indecision. cam2 (the corridor's hardest, at 25 fps) stays the worst even after every defense.

The data-driven leg join

The one thing that must generalize across cameras is the map between our leg IDs and Miovision's approach names — without it we can't compare to ground truth. It's derived, not hand-maintained: od_accuracy.leg_idx(camera) matches each leg's cardinal_direction (N/S/E/W) to the cardinal prefix (NB/SB/EB/WB) of the Miovision approach name. 

leg L38 cardinal "E"  ──→  Miovision approach "EB Barnes Bridge Rd"
leg L39 cardinal "N"  ──→  Miovision approach "NB N Belt Line Rd"

This handles 3-leg T's (9 movements) and 4-ways (16) the same way, with no per-intersection table — so build_bank.py, apply_bank.py, apply_hybrid.py, and od_accuracy.py all take --camera N and just work.

Get the cardinals right. Because the join is by cardinal direction, a mislabeled leg is silent and corrosive: cam1's legs were once 180°-swapped (NB↔SB, EB↔WB), which mismeasured every count and flipped the exported TMC directions until it was caught. The cardinal labels are load-bearing.

Stitching the corridor together

Adjacent cameras can see the same vehicle (overlapping fields of view between intersections). The cross-camera dedup in the aggregator collapses those parallel-overlap duplicates so a car driving the corridor is counted once per intersection it passes through, not twice in the overlap.

That's the whole system — from a calibrated still frame to a five-intersection corridor of validated turning-movement counts, running entirely on a local machine. The interesting frontier from here is the same as everywhere in this app: drive the per-camera net error down, one measured change at a time.
