7. Accuracy & Validation

The pipeline is judged against a commercial ground truth (Miovision) and tuned until it agrees. This page is the measurement discipline and the specific defenses that close the gap — the part of the system that turns "it runs" into "it's right."

The metric

Ground truth is the Miovision per-minute origin→destination matrix. The headline number is net error at TMC granularity: for each 15-minute per-movement cell, |ours − Miovision|, summed and normalized. We report net (not per-minute gross) because the deliverable is a 15-minute TMC, and minute-level timing jitter — a vehicle Miovision logs at 07:14:59 and we log at 07:15:01 — isn't a real error at that granularity.

TermMeaning
net errordisagreement at 15-min granularity — the deliverable's accuracy
gross errorper-minute disagreement — always higher; the gap to net is timing + compensating over/under counts
hiddena cell that's right on net but wrong on gross (an over-count cancels an under-count)
≤1% is aspirational, not a floor. On busy approaches the timing jitter alone, plus Miovision's own counting noise, sets a floor above zero. The goal is to match Miovision within that noise, not to beat it. scripts/od_accuracy.py --camera N prints the per-movement table.

The iteration loop

The detection cache makes tuning fast: detection runs once; every tracker/bank/attribution experiment retracks the cached detections in minutes. So the loop is tight:

1build_bank.py — derive the bank from cached tracks + Miovision OD
2apply_bank.py / apply_hybrid.py — retrack the window with that bank (no re-detection)
3od_accuracy.py — measure net error per movement
4compare A/B, keep the winner, repeat

Defense 1 — the snap-magnet

Reading origin/movement off a matched path (page 4) fails badly when a bank path is geometrically straight: a "turn" polyline that runs straight down the arterial will shape-match the through stream and swallow it, rewriting hundreds of throughs into a turn with manual volume 6.

Three data-driven gates kill it, none needing a per-camera flag:

  • Build-time magnet gate — reject a turn path that is straight (chord/arc > 0.95) and over-supported (> 3× manual volume).
  • Origin-rewrite gate (attribution, page 4) — refuse to rewrite a straight track's origin to a non-nearest leg (straightness ≥ 0.97). cam4 22.7% → 7.0% net.
  • Straight-recovery gate — reject a heading-recovered turn that's straight, even at low support (the build-time gate's over-support condition can't catch it).

Defense 2 — recovering dropped movements

The bank is built by grouping tracks on the nearest leg anchor to each endpoint. That silently drops any movement whose tracks start and end near the same anchor — a right turn that queues by the cross-street, say. cam5's EB→NB right was tracked 36 times but binned to a nonsense (NB,NB) cell, so the bank had no path and the movement read 1 of 39.

Heading recovery re-bins those tracks by their direction of travel (entry bearing ≈ origin's heading; exit bearing ≈ reverse of the destination's). It only fires for a real Miovision cell that nearest-anchor left under-supported — so it never overrides a working cell, and is a no-op on cameras whose anchors are fine (cam5 EB→NB recovered 1→16; cam2's dead EB-through 1→38).

Defense 3 — duplication

A 25 fps camera double-boxes and ID-switches the same vehicle, inflating throughs. A strict class-agnostic NMS before tracking (PRE_TRACK_NMS_IOU = 0.85) collapses the duplicates: cam2 dropped from 29.8% → 20.2% net with the duplicated NB/SB throughs alone fixed. It ships default-off and is enabled per-camera, because on lower-fps cameras it over-merges genuinely-separate vehicles.

The discipline: per-camera measurement rules everything

Every "global" lever flipped sign on some camera. Fresh throughs helped cam5 and regressed cam4. A pure heading-grouped bank helped cam1 and broke cam3. NMS helps 25 fps and hurts 10 fps. There is no corridor-wide knob — so nothing is applied to a camera until it's measured on that camera.

The rules that follow from that:

  • A/B is the ultimate gate. Build-time gates just stop the worst paths reaching it; the clean A/B (bank ± one change, identical pipeline) is what decides. Build-time support can be low while apply-time capture is catastrophic — only the A/B sees that.
  • Engineer visual gate + atomic backup before any write to project.db. Every apply backs up first.
  • Pick the tracker per camera by measurement — see the hybrid on page 2 and the corridor on page 8.
  • No silent caps. If a step drops data (a gate, a window limit), it's logged, not swallowed.

Offline replay harnesses

Because every event stores its trajectory and classifier_* diagnostics, fixes can be validated offline without re-running detection: scripts/replay_snapmagnet.py, replay_dedup.py, and the overnight overnight_accuracy.py orchestrator re-score stored trajectories under a proposed change and report the net-error delta.
