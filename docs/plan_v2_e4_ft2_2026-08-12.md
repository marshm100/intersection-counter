  Report per-window delta, compliant/scored pairs (cells_scored differs

- **G-E4b (disposition):** ft2 re-enters candidacy at a camera only if
  ≥ +1.0 on ≥ 2 of that camera's windows AND ≥ 0 on the rest of them.
  Otherwise the 2026-07-24 disposition STANDS, re-earned under today's
  chain, and E4 is closed honestly (no longer "measured before assembly
  existed").
- **Activation contingency (pre-registered — the G-A3 confound shape):**
  ft2 shifts detections → origin-evidence coverage shifts → the activation
  precondition (default ON, bar 0.45) can flip the gate+posterior pair
  between an arm and its control. Control states on record: cam2 ACTIVATED
  0.564/0.487/0.485; cam1 NOT 0.265/0.438 (0700 relevant here); cam4 NOT
  0.304/0.442/0.318; cam5 NOT 0.330/0.441/0.402. Margins at cam4/cam5
  study_1100 are −0.008/−0.009 — live flip risk. RULE: record
  `result.evidence_activation` from every arm sidecar; any arm whose
  activated state ≠ its control's makes the default-flags comparison VOID
  at that window; run supplementary arm + control with
  `EVIDENCE_ACTIVATION_ENABLED=0` (fresh workdirs, stems `e4ft2x_*` /
  `e4ctrlx_*`) and evaluate G-E4a/b on the flag-off pair, reporting the
  decomposition both ways (the v2_confound_table.py method). No undeclared
  fourth variable.
