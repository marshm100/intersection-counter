 15-MINUTE INTERVAL ANALYSIS - Manual vs Miovision vs Our App
 Sunnyvale, TX corridor  |  generated 2026-06-29
================================================================================

 METHOD
   Ground truth is Simeon Lewis' per-minute manual hand count; both Miovision
   and our app are measured against it. The manual sheets are only partially
   filled, so for each 15-min bin all three sources are summed over EXACTLY the
   minutes the human counted in that bin (apples-to-apples, same minutes).

 COLUMN DEFINITIONS
   interval  - start of the 15-minute wall-clock bin.
   min       - minutes within the bin the manual counter actually filled in; all
               three sources are summed over exactly these minutes. Fewer than
               15 is marked "(partial Nm)".
   MANUAL    - human hand-count total over those minutes (THE GROUND TRUTH).
   MIOVIS    - Miovision's count over the same minutes.
   OURS      - our app's count over the same minutes.
   Mio err   - signed error of Miovision vs MANUAL = (MIOVIS - MANUAL)/MANUAL.
   Ours err  - signed error of our app  vs MANUAL = (OURS  - MANUAL)/MANUAL.
               Sign: negative = UNDER-count, positive = OVER-count.

 SUMMARY-ROW DEFINITIONS
   TOTAL     - sum of every bin; its err% is the volume-weighted NET error
               (each interval contributes in proportion to its volume).
   AVG err   - unweighted MEAN of the per-interval SIGNED errors (every interval
               weighted equally). Over- and under-counts have opposite signs and
               partially CANCEL, so this stays near the net and UNDERSTATES how
               wrong any single interval is.
   AVG |err| - MEAN ABSOLUTE ERROR: mean of the per-interval errors after taking
               absolute value. Measures the typical SIZE of a 15-min error
               regardless of sign - the honest per-interval accuracy metric;
               cancellation is removed.
   Both AVG rows use FULL bins only (>=10 counted min) so 1-min boundary bins
   (where a source's processing window does not cover the edge minute) do not
   distort them.

 REFERENCE
   The TxDOT count-accuracy tolerance referenced is 5%: an accepted TMC count
   should hold <=5% error. NET (TOTAL) error can pass while per-interval error
   (AVG |err|) fails - which is the point of this report.

================================================================================
 KEY FINDING - net accuracy hides per-interval error
================================================================================

 At the TOTAL level our app is excellent (-1.5% / +2.2% net) and on par with
 Miovision. But AVG |err| (mean absolute per-interval error) tells the real
 story - cam1: Miovision 2.8% vs ours 7.1%. Our typical 15-min error is ~2.5x
 Miovision's because our errors SWING (under-count late AM, over-count PM) and
 only CANCEL in the net.

 So several of our 15-min intervals exceed the 5% bar while Miovision stays
 under it. NET is not a sufficient acceptance metric - per-interval consistency
 (AVG |err|) is the real target.

 Caveats:
   - cam1 is the substantive dataset (~16 full intervals, AM+PM peaks). cam2's
     manual stops at 07:35, so only ~2 full intervals (averages not robust).
   - Tool: scripts/triangulate_intervals.py (re-runnable).
